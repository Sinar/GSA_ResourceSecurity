# Global South Alliance (GSA) Research Workflow

### **Phase 1: Project Initiation & Data Procurement (Month 1: March 2026)**
* Commence project operations on March 16, 2026.
* Procure necessary satellite data and computing equipment.
* Utilize the allocated $1,300 budget for geospatial data and cloud GPU resources.
* Initialize the local development environment using standard analysis stacks (Python, Pandas, VS Code) to manage the data ingestion and modeling pipeline. 

### **Phase 2: Geospatial & Policy Core Analysis (Months 1–4: March – June 2026)**
* Execute Computer Vision (CV) modeling on the acquired satellite imagery.
* Generate a geospatial dataset of data center footprints with modeled resource estimates.
* Conduct a policy audit of Malaysia’s Data Centre Framework.
* Perform a targeted comparative desk review of data center governance in a key Global South region, such as Indonesia, to contextualize findings.
* Submit the Interim Report by June 15, 2026.
* Ensure the initial CV analysis and policy audit draft are completed by this milestone.

### **Phase 3: Synthesis & Tool Development (Months 4–5: June – August 2026)**
* Synthesize the technical and policy findings into public-facing tools.
* Produce a visual evidence library of satellite overlays.
* Draft a comprehensive data-driven "Resource Security" public policy report.
* Submit the Preliminary Results Report by August 2026.

### **Phase 4: Knowledge Consolidation & Advocacy Launch (Months 5–6: August – September 2026)**
* Finalize and publicly launch all project findings.
* Draft an open-access journal article situating the Malaysian case within comparative digital colonialism discourse.
* Utilize the $2,200 budget allocated for Open Access Publication Fees.
* Submit the Final Report by September 15, 2026.

### **Phase 5: Post-Project Dissemination (October 2026)**
* Prepare presentation slides and materials for the GSA Virtual Seminar.
* Deliver the presentation at the GSA Virtual Seminar on October 14, 2026.
